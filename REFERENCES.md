# References

The following primary sources provide the public research context for geometry
reasoning with diagrams and text. This archive does not redistribute their
code, datasets, or model artifacts.

These works established the task formulations, datasets, and comparison points
that made these experiments possible. The visual-input ablations summarized
in this archive are intended to complement their benchmark evaluations.

1. Jiaqi Chen, Tong Li, Jinghui Qin, Pan Lu, Liang Lin, Chongyu Chen, and
   Xiaodan Liang. “UniGeo: Unifying Geometry Logical Reasoning via Reformulating
   Mathematical Expression.” *arXiv preprint arXiv:2212.02746*, 2022.
   [Paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2212.02746) ·
   [DOI](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2212.02746)

2. Jiaqi Chen, Jianheng Tang, Jinghui Qin, Xiaodan Liang, Lingbo Liu, Eric Xing,
   and Liang Lin. “GeoQA: A Geometric Question Answering Benchmark Towards
   Multimodal Numerical Reasoning.” *Findings of the Association for
   Computational Linguistics: ACL-IJCNLP 2021*, pages 513–523, 2021.
   [ACL Anthology](https://aclanthology.org/2021.findings-acl.46/) ·
   [DOI](https://doi.org/10.18653/v1/2021.findings-acl.46)

3. Pan Lu, Ran Gong, Shibiao Jiang, Liang Qiu, Siyuan Huang, Xiaodan Liang, and
   Song-Chun Zhu. “Inter-GPS: Interpretable Geometry Problem Solving with Formal
   Language and Symbolic Reasoning.” *Proceedings of ACL-IJCNLP 2021*, pages
   6774–6786, 2021.
   [ACL Anthology](https://aclanthology.org/2021.acl-long.528/) ·
   [DOI](https://doi.org/10.18653/v1/2021.acl-long.528)
