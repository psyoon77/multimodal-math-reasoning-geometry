# Contribution and Attribution Boundary

## Project-level work represented

This archive represents work across five research activities:

1. framing experimental questions for geometry problems containing diagrams
   and written descriptions;
2. developing multimodal research prototypes;
3. comparing system behavior under controlled experimental conditions;
4. performing visual-input sensitivity and behavioral analysis; and
5. documenting limitations and unresolved research questions.

The public description intentionally stops at the activity level. It does not
disclose implementation choices, exact experimental procedures, private
findings, or materials needed to reproduce the work.

## External-work boundary

The benchmarks, publications, and general research foundations listed in
`REFERENCES.md` remain the work of their respective authors. Their inclusion as
references does not imply contribution to or affiliation with this archive.

No third-party code, dataset, pretrained artifact, or model weight is included
in this public release.

The cited works established the task formulations, benchmarks, and baselines
that enabled the experiments summarized here. The project's input ablations
are presented as a complementary evaluation perspective that builds on that
prior work.

## Claim boundary

This archive preserves two aggregate top-10 accuracy comparisons transcribed
from the historical report. It does not independently reproduce those values or
make a current leaderboard, production, or reproducibility claim. The supporting
technical materials are not distributed.
